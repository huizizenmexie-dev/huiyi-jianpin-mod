All ten product images were edited with the built-in image-generation tool using the original supplied or previously generated photos as edit targets. The edit removes the photographic backdrop and reframes to a transparent square. Original inputs remain in `design-assets/source-products/` and `design-assets/generated/`. Reviewed cutouts are preserved in `design-assets/product-cutouts/`.

The resulting photos are illustrative catalogue assets, not evidence of packaging, certifications or manufacturing facilities. No business claims were added. Labels and ingredient colors were kept as closely as possible to the supplied references. Fine details can vary in AI-edited assets; keep original photos for comparison.

## Responsive delivery

`ResponsiveImage` provides AVIF and WebP choices. Product images: 400, 800 and 1200 square. Editorial images: 480×320, 960×640, 1536×1024. Only the selected candidate downloads. The hero is eager/high priority; offscreen images are lazy. All product surfaces are pure white. Dimensions are reserved before images load.

`node scripts/optimize-media.mjs ./tmp/media-tools/node_modules/sharp` regenerates variants from approved PNG masters after installing optional Sharp tooling as documented in the script. This is a maintenance operation; regular site builds use committed image variants and do not need Sharp. `docs/design/media-manifest.json` records resulting dimensions, size and source. Font sources/licenses: `docs/design/font-manifest.md`.
